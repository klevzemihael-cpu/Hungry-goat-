Zakaj Hungry Goat?Why Hungry Goat?

Hungry pomeni lakoto po napredku in tudi to, da pri meni ne boš stradal. Goat je gorska koza, ki pleza tam, kjer drugi obstanejo, in hkrati GOAT: najboljša verzija sebe. Hungry means hungry for progress, and also that you won’t go hungry on my plans. Goat is the mountain goat that climbs where others stop, and the GOAT: the best version of yourself.

Sem licenciran osebni trener iz Maribora in treniram v TNT Gym. Delam z ljudmi, ki nimajo časa za ure v fitnesu in ne marajo ekstremnih diet, pa vseeno hočejo vidne rezultate. I’m a licensed personal trainer from Maribor, training at TNT Gym. I work with people who don’t have hours for the gym and hate crash diets, but still want visible results.

FAQ

Pogosta vprašanjaCommon questions

Sem popoln začetnik. Je to zame?I’m a complete beginner. Is this for me?

Absolutno. Večina mojih strank začne z nič. Program prilagodim tvoji trenutni formi, poškodbam in urniku.Absolutely. Most of my clients start from zero. The program is adapted to your current fitness, injuries and schedule.

Ali moram biti član TNT Gym?Do I need a TNT Gym membership?

Za osebne treninge in skupinske vadbe se dogovoriva ob posvetu. Za online coaching lahko treniraš v kateremkoli fitnesu ali doma.For 1-on-1 and group sessions we’ll sort it out during the consultation. For online coaching you can train at any gym or at home.

Bom moral jesti samo piščanca in riž?Will I have to eat only chicken and rice?

Ne. Ime je Hungry Goat z razlogom. Ješ normalno hrano, ki jo imaš rad, v pravih količinah. Tudi pico.No. It’s called Hungry Goat for a reason. You eat normal food you like, in the right amounts. Pizza included.

Koliko časa na teden potrebujem?How much time do I need per week?

Že 3 treningi po 45–60 minut na teden prinesejo odlične rezultate.Just 3 sessions of 45–60 minutes per week bring great results.

Kdaj bom videl rezultate?When will I see results?

Večina strank opazi razliko v energiji in moči v 2–3 tednih, vidne spremembe na telesu pa v 6–8 tednih.Most clients feel a difference in energy and strength within 2–3 weeks, with visible body changes in 6–8 weeks.
